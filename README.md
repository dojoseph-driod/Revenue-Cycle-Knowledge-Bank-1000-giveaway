# Revenue Cycle Knowledge Bank
 
## 📚 About the Project
 
Revenue Cycle Knowledge Bank is a free educational platform dedicated to sharing knowledge, resources, tools, and best practices related to Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM).
 
The goal of this initiative is to support healthcare professionals, aspiring RCM leaders, and students by providing accessible learning materials, practical guides, and industry insights.
 
🔗 Website: https://dojoseph-driod.github.io/RevCycle-Knowledge-Bank/
 
---
 
## 🎯 Mission
 
To make Revenue Cycle Management knowledge accessible, practical, and easy to understand for everyone in the healthcare ecosystem.
